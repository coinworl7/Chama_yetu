Sources: `chamasoft.com`, `tekeleza.com`, `chamapro.co.ke`, `chamasystems.com`, `fibo360.co.ke`.

## What that means

1. **You cannot win on price.** Someone is already at KSh 500/month. Undercutting a KSh 500
   competitor with no capital is how you get a hundred users and no income.
2. **You cannot win on features.** Every competitor has loans, fines and merry-go-round. They have
   had years to build it.
3. **The gap is adoption, not software.** A 15-member chama will not enter twelve months of history
   into a new system, and will not ask every member to install an app. Those groups are currently
   served by nothing.
4. **Per-member pricing leaves large groups unhappy.** KSh 100 × 30 members = KSh 3,000/month for
   the same work as a 10-member group. Flat pricing is a talking point.

So the position is narrow and honest:

> **We do not replace your system. We produce the statement your members can trust, from the records
> you already have.** One page, monthly, reconciled against M-Pesa.

If a group genuinely wants to migrate, refer them to Chamasoft. You lose a deal and gain the
reputation of someone who does not oversell.

## Price ladder

| Offer | Price | Includes | Why this number |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Statement, one-off** | **KSh 500** | One month, one revision, PDF + WhatsApp summary | Half a month of Chamasoft, and less than the average disputed contribution |
| Setup + history | KSh 3,000 | Opening balances, member list, three months of back statements | Pays for the two hours of data entry you will actually do |
| **Monthly, up to 30 members** | **KSh 900** | Statement by the 5th, one revision, WhatsApp summary, arrears list | Just under Chamasoft, flat regardless of member count |
| Annual prepay | KSh 9,000 | 12 months | Ten months' price, cash upfront |
| Done-for-you bookkeeping | KSh 3,000–5,000/month | You keep the ledger, chase arrears, prepare the statement | Your income while the product matures |

**Never discount.** If they push back, remove scope: no WhatsApp summary, or statement only every
second month. A discount tells them the first price was fiction.

## Terms to say out loud

- 50% upfront for setup, the rest on delivery.
- Statement delivered by the 5th of the following month.
- One revision included; further changes are a new statement.
- They own their data; you will export it on request and delete it 90 days after they leave.
- You are not handling their money — no collection, no custody, no payouts. Ever. Say this early.

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Chamasoft is only 1,000." | "It is, and it's good software. It also needs every member to sign up and a year of history entered. I'm not asking you to migrate — I'm producing the statement from the records you already have." |
| "We already have a treasurer." | "Good — this is for the treasurer. It's the two hours at month end that we're removing." |
| "Can it be free?" | "The first one is free so you can judge it. After that it's 500 a month, which is less than one member's contribution." |
| "Send me the app link." | "There's no app yet. You send me the records, I send back the statement. If enough groups want the app, I'll build it." |
| "Our books are private." | "They are. I sign a one-page confidentiality note, I only use the records to prepare your statement, and I delete them when you stop." |
| "Let me ask the chair." | "Of course. Can I send you the sample so you have something to show them?" |

## Revenue math

| Groups | At KSh 900/month | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | KSh 4,500 | One accountant partner, or your own network |
| 20 | KSh 18,000 | Roughly 6 months of consistent outreach |
| 60 | KSh 54,000 | Beyond manual production — the product has to do the statements |

The crossover is around **25–30 groups**: past that you cannot produce statements by hand in the
first week of the month. That is when the code gets written, not before.
